# Changelog

What changed in each version of Quite Handy. The section for a version is shown in the update dialog,
so write it for people using the app.



## 2.3

Everything new since 2.0, at a glance:

- Rebuilt under the hood: Quite Handy runs faster and steadier, with far less work while it waits
- Import & export: Share a tool, your whole toolset or a pointer as a .quitehandy file. Drag it onto Settings or double-click it to add it
- Custom colors: A color grid with 10 hues in 8 shades, your own colors per tool, colors from a picture, and Vivid or Pastel without losing them
- Copy colors from one tool and paste them on another
- Text: Glass or colored, with custom colors for the text or its background
- Emojis: New defaults, and the size with a slider or by scrolling, in finer steps
- Glitter: A new tool that sprays sparkling flakes
- Pointers: Favorites, a random pointer per session, and your own pointers from any picture, with a stick added or stretched
- Toolbar: Always shown, near the pointer or with a hotkey
- Restore your tools with Tab: Everything changed in a session goes back to your settings
- Screencasts: Start with a window or an area, a smoother start, and a webcam you can drag, resize or throw into a corner
- Drag & drop: Drop tool files and pictures right where they go
- A new app icon, Quite Handy in the Dock, and a problem report in Settings › About
- One look all over Settings: Buttons, dropdowns, dialogs, tooltips and notes match in light and dark mode



## 2.2.3

- Fixes high CPU use while Quite Handy was idle, and the stutter while drawing that came with it (since 2.1.5)
- Problem report: Long stalls are recorded with what the app was doing at that moment
- Text handles end at a whiteboard's edge, like the text
- Everything stacks in the order it was made. Whatever you grab comes to the top



## 2.2.1

- Problem report in Settings › About: Start logging writes down how Quite Handy runs on your Mac, with stalls, slow frames, CPU, GPU, memory, heat, power, screens and permissions. It goes on after a crash or a restart. Nothing is sent automatically: Stop & copy log puts it on the clipboard as plain text, to read and send yourself



## 2.2

- Favorites: Mark cursors with the heart in Choose cursor. They show in their own group at the top
- Random cursor and Random favorite cursor: Every session starts with another cursor for that tool
- The chosen cursor has a blue outline, and Choose cursor scrolls smoothly
- A new color grid with 10 hues in 8 shades. Vivid, Pastel and the rainbow all come from it
- Colors in Settings: Click the chosen color to replace it from the grid or with sliders. Backspace removes a color and a + adds one again
- ⌘C and ⌘V over a color copy and paste it: As hex, from an SVG or from a picture
- Use colors from clipboard image: The six main colors of a picture become a tool's colors
- Reset to Vivid or Pastel in the colors' menu brings back all of a tool's colors
- Text: The chosen color as the text or as its background, set per tool in Settings
- Emojis: New defaults ❤️ 🧠 💪 ❌, an optional circle behind them, and scroll or swipe over an emoji to resize it
- Restore your tools with Tab: Sizes, colors, emojis and opacity changed in a session go back to your settings. Press it twice to switch to your first tool too
- Shortcuts: A single key like Tab works for everything that only works in a session. ⇧, ⌃, ⌥ and ⌘ on their own are no longer shortcuts. Backspace clears any shortcut
- Name your screenshots and screencasts as you like, in Settings under Naming
- Copied screenshots paste into the Finder too
- Pressing a screenshot shortcut again starts the selection over, and the size in the loupe is in points
- Screencasts: The webcam shows right away while you pick a window or an area and takes a third of it. Record is white on red. The shortcut starts over and never stops a recording
- Tooltips slide in
- The toolbar no longer flashes when Quite Handy starts



## 2.1.6

- Glitter: a quick click is a tap, as with the pen. Hold or move to spray
- Numbers: the next number shows only once you let go of the one you placed



## 2.1.5

- Shortcuts show only what the tools you use need, and the shortcuts of tools you turned off no longer take their keys

- A window you record gets the red frame too, and it moves with the window
- Record starts recording right away, while a red circle shows where Stop is in the menu bar
- Choose the corner the webcam starts in: bottom right, bottom left, top left or top right. It docks to the same corner of a window or an area
- The recording controls always stay above the webcam and the red frame
- Esc and a right-click no longer reach the app underneath, when they cancel a screencast or end a session
- Make your own cursor: Test is now Try out

- Try a tool with its play button: it now stays on that tool
- A new tool shows in the toolbar right away, with the simple cursor until you pick one
- A new marker starts vibrant yellow at 30 %, and its simple cursor is its slanted tip
- Choose cursor shows five larger cursors per row, with more room between them
- Make your own cursor: the crosshair spans the whole picture, and the loupe fades in as you come near
- The toolbar sits a little higher above the Dock by default
- After a screencast, the recording plays right away
- Whiteboards are on from the start
- A new About page
- After this update, Quite Handy starts fresh one last time: the onboarding shows again, and all settings and shortcuts are back to their defaults. Your own cursors stay



## 2.1.3

- New installs now start with the intended defaults: Scribble and Flashlight on, the other tools off, and the shortcuts and tools as Quite Handy comes
- Text starts on a colored background at 90 % with pastel colors, and Clack is the new default tap sound
- Screenshots and Screencasts explain what they do, even while they are off
- Each screen tool asks for screen recording with its own explanation

## 2.1.2

- Show toolbar now has three options: Always, Near cursor and With hotkey
- Near cursor is the new default. The toolbar appears when you move the cursor near it and hides again when you move away
- With hotkey keeps the toolbar hidden. Hold its hotkey to open the toolbar right under the cursor, with the tool, color or emoji you picked last
- The Show/hide toolbar shortcut and the Toolbar item in the menu bar are gone. Choose how the toolbar shows in Settings
- All shortcuts and hold keys are back to the new defaults once after this update. Your tools keep their own shortcuts



## 2.1.1

- Text: pick Glass or Colored right in the tool's settings. Colored now puts your color behind the text, with the text in a light or dark tone of it
- Sleep, lock screen, screensaver and shutdown now end everything – drawing, flashlight, screenshots, OCR, screencasts and presentation. A running screencast is saved to your desktop
- Whiteboards close whenever a session ends. Restore last session brings them back
- Screenshots and OCR no longer make videos in your browser blink, for example in a Google Meet call
- 9 new tap sounds: blip, chirp, clack, coin, pew, quack, snap, tock and typewriter
- Shortcut fields spell out their keys when you hover them, and a red field names the shortcut it conflicts with
- New default shortcuts: ⌃⇥ for Scribble, ⌃⌥⇥ for Flashlight, H for the toolbar, S and A for colors, W and Q for tools



## 2.1

Quite Handy is now a complete toolkit for showing things on your screen. Turn on the tools you need in Settings and leave the rest off.

- **Point & draw** – a hand on a stick with pen, arrow, marker, emojis, numbers, text, pixelate and glitter, plus a flashlight that zooms in and whiteboards on top of your screen
- **Capture** – screenshots of the screen, an area, a window or a long scrolling page, screencasts with your voice and webcam, and text copied from anywhere with OCR
- **Present** – presentation mode keeps your Mac awake, hides the desktop and runs your own shortcuts when you start and stop
- **Make it yours** – more than 40 cursors or your own from any picture, a shortcut for every tool and 15 tap sounds

## 1.7.0

- Scrolling screenshots with ⌥⌘6: draw an area, then scroll down slowly and the page is put together into one long picture
- Text recognition (OCR) with ⌥⌘2: drag around text and it is copied
- Screencasts with ⌥⌘7: record the full screen, a window or an area, with your voice and a webcam bubble; Stop in the menu bar
- Hands: each hand has a tool – draw, mark, stamp or pixelate – and the emoji picker searches as you type
- Every settings page shows its shortcuts, and macOS's own screenshot shortcuts are one click away
- New default shortcuts: ⌥Space turns the hand on and off, ⌥⇧Space the flashlight (shortcuts you changed yourself stay as they are)
- Updates now come from esreverse.com

## 1.6.0

- Hand Pointer is now called Quite Handy. It is a new app on your Mac: allow Accessibility once more in System Settings, and Open at login is now on by default

- One undo history for everything: lines, stamps and whiteboards
- Screenshots: ⌘C copies and ⌘S saves the screen, or the whiteboard under the pointer, without the hand
- Flashlight: Hold Tab for a spotlight that darkens the rest of the screen; turn on Zoom to magnify inside it. Swipe to change its size and zoom, or hold ⌥ and move the mouse
- Space and Tab can now be used as hold keys, like ⇧ ⌃ ⌥ ⌘
- Opacity and size knobs in the toolbar, and keys to step through the ink colours (V and C)
- The glove draws in black and white so its lines show on any background
- Number stamps use a rounder font, grow while you hold, and go up to 9
- New defaults: R clears, ⌘Z and ⇧⌘Z undo and redo, ⇧ stamps, T shows the toolbar
- Settings are reorganised with a new Toolbar page, and Esc always ends the session

## 1.1.0

First release of Tiny Pointer, based on TinyHand 1.0.2 by Kamran Ahmed.

- Rainbow ink: Lines run through every colour, with adjustable speed and a hue shift for each new line
- Stroke size and line opacity can be set separately from the pointer size
- Tap circle: A circle grows and fades out where you tap
- Tap sound plays on tap only, or on tap and draw
- Zoom loupe: Hold a key to magnify the screen around the pointer
- Ink colours sit right in the toolbar
- The toolbar can be moved with the handle on its left; right-click the handle to put it back
- The toolbar can be shown or hidden, and gets out of the way while you interact with your Mac
- New shortcuts for clearing, colours, switching pointers, sound and the toolbar; single keys work while the pointer is on
- Optional: Right-click turns the pointer off
- Settings reorganised into General, Shortcuts, Draw, Zoom, Pointers, Sound and About
- Updates are checked and installed automatically
